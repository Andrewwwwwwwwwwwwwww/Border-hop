🗺️ How to Play Border Hop

Travel overland from the Starting Country to the Target Destination by hopping across shared land borders.

  • Three Difficulty Levels:
    • Easy: Contiguous mainland borders only, shorter 3–5 hop routes, and auto-zooms the map to your region.
    • Medium: Contiguous mainland borders only with longer 5–8 hop routes and a global view.
    • Hard: Full political borders including overseas territories & exclaves (e.g., France ↔ Brazil/Suriname via French Guiana, Spain ↔ Morocco via Ceuta/Melilla, Russia ↔ Poland/Lithuania via Kaliningrad).
  • Two Scoring Modes: Switch between Least Countries (fewest hops) and Shortest Distance (minimum cumulative km between country centroids, preventing cheap giant jumps across Russia or China).
  • Scoring (out of 100): In Least Countries you start with 100 points: −10 for each hop more than the best possible, −10 for each wrong guess (a country that doesn't share a land border), and −15 for each hint. In Shortest Distance your score is 100 × the shortest route's km ÷ your km, and each wrong guess or hint adds 1,000 km.
  • Map: Tap a country on the map, then Hop here, to move there instead of using the list.
  • Daily: One route per day, the same for everyone (Medium, Least Countries), with the same choices at every step. A new route starts at midnight US Pacific time. Every hop counts, even ones you step back from, and your progress is saved if you close the page. Finish it each day to build a streak.
  • Practice: As many random routes as you like, with your own difficulty, scoring and region. Practice never affects your daily streak.
